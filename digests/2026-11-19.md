# My Luma scout: 2026-11-19

Upstream moved from v3.6.2 to v3.10.0 in a day: four minor releases plus several patches. Two watched repos changed (couch-tv 0.13.2 to 0.13.6, Aerial 3.1.0 and 3.1.1), and eight new home screen repos turned up. The webosbrew catalogue has had no new commits since 2026-11-17, and Reddit searches found nothing new.

## Merge from upstream?

- **Centre the Home dock when it holds 1 to 5 apps (v3.9.0, PR #340, 5e4c33a)**: https://github.com/ecklf/lg-luma/pull/340. When there are fewer than six apps, the dock shrinks to fit and centres itself, and the cards keep their usual size. My Luma's favourites row has the same left-aligned look when it's short, so this is a small visual fit.
- **Down does nothing when the drawer would be empty (v3.9.0, PR #339, f180d10)**: https://github.com/ecklf/lg-luma/pull/339. Focus and widgets now stay put instead of opening an empty app drawer. In My Luma, Down opens the drawer, so this is relevant whenever every app is a favourite or hidden. It's a small, low-risk merge.
- **Game controller navigation (v3.8.0, bd418ae)**: https://github.com/ecklf/lg-luma/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md. This adds SDL game controller support (including the DualSense where webOS recognises it): D-pad or left stick to move, OK and Back holds, Options, and hot-plugging. My Luma has no controller input at all. It's worth taking if a controller is ever paired to the G5.
- **USB storage in Inputs (v3.10.0, PR #347, 8e73bc6)**: https://github.com/ecklf/lg-luma/pull/347. Upstream lists the USB drives webOS reports in Inputs, lets you rename or hide them, and opens LG's media browser on select. That is an app launch, not a system control. My Luma only uses "USB" as an input-icon label. Upstream itself says it still needs hardware testing.
- **TV performance batch (v3.7.0 to v3.8.2)**: https://github.com/ecklf/lg-luma/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md. It decodes first-scene and dock images on worker threads, reuses linked shaders and decoded images between launches, uses VAOs for logo, text and paint draws, prepares Settings and drawer shadows ahead of time while Home is idle, and paces animations correctly without VSync (b22b4fc). It also stops the "Opening" toast on TV and asks for the app list once per Home press instead of twice. This is the biggest win for snappiness, but it touches the renderer heavily and is likely to conflict with My Luma's own renderer and damage changes. Cherry-pick it in pieces.
- **Readable focused pills in Settings (v3.10.0, 867cd5b)**: https://github.com/ecklf/lg-luma/pull/351. The focused label gets a dark opaque fill with a white outline instead of a bright white fill. It's a small contrast fix, and taking it depends on whether My Luma's quick panel has the same white-on-white issue.
- **Skip: Midnight-only wallpaper and a static splash (v3.8.3, 48bc9e0, 50cda86, a62a16b)**. Upstream removed its other bundled wallpapers and the splashes that matched each wallpaper. Merging this would cost My Luma its wallpapers and its splash_background feature.

## Worth borrowing

- **Glass icon style switch (MoLakaz TV)**: https://github.com/benosss/molakaz-tv. One setting picks the icon style: each app as a white mark on dark glass, glass tinted in the app's colour, or the original icons. This would give My Luma's favourites row a uniform look as an option, with original icons staying the default.
- **Ambient sounds that follow the weather (MoLakaz TV)**: https://github.com/benosss/molakaz-tv. Optional background sound: birds at dawn, waves by day, rain when it is raining. My Luma already has weather effects, location and UI sounds, so an opt-in ambient sound for ambient mode would build on what's there. MoLakaz also captions each background with its place name and a fact about it.
- **Crash guard back to stock Home (MoLakaz TV)**: https://github.com/benosss/molakaz-tv. If the launcher crashes three times within five minutes, the TV goes back to LG's home screen on its own. It's worth checking whether My Luma's boot and lifecycle path has an equivalent safety net.
- **Focused app's own background as the wallpaper (swenz custom launcher)**: https://github.com/swenz/webos-custom-launcher. When an app with a `bgImage` in its appinfo is focused (Netflix, Disney+ and others), that image fills the screen, a bit like tvOS Top Shelf. As an option in My Luma's favourites row it needs no network, since it only reads app metadata.
- **Live theme preview (Homeboard)**: https://github.com/Shaku-Med/lg-tv-launcher. Moving over a theme or colour scheme previews it live, OK keeps it and Back cancels. The same pattern could work for My Luma's wallpaper tint and clock position choices.
- **Clock font choice (Aerial v3.1.0)**: https://github.com/aabytt/custom-screensaver-aerial/pull/98. Aerial now lets you choose the on-screen clock font. My Luma's glass clock offers size and position settings but no font setting. Aerial's same release adds "solar mode" playlists split into sunrise, day, sunset and night, which adds weight to the four-period day/night idea from 2026-11-18.
- **Night clock in dim amber, and a morning brief (TeleDash Pro)**: https://github.com/WMSDev1/TeleDash-Pro. At night the idle view switches to a drifting clock in very dark amber-red, and during set morning hours it shows a full-brightness summary of the day. Only the colour and schedule part fits, because TeleDash also turns the TV's picture down, which is a system control. This could be an ambient "night palette" in My Luma.

## Already have

- **Show app names (upstream v3.7.0, d9511c1)**: My Luma already has `show_app_names` and `show_home_names`.
- **Homeboard ambient slideshow, weather and themes**: https://github.com/Shaku-Med/lg-tv-launcher. This is a web app with idle photo slideshow, Open-Meteo weather and layouts, all of which My Luma covers natively.
- **zipDash**: https://github.com/ZIPender/zipDash. A quiet native Qt home for webOS 6 with apps, inputs and favourites. My Luma does all of that.
- **TeleDash countdowns, drifting idle clock**: https://github.com/WMSDev1/TeleDash-Pro. These are covered by holiday reminders (4.9.0) and pixel shift in ambient mode.
- **couch-tv guest Welcome (0.13.3, 205c451)**: https://github.com/Seagey/couch-tv. The greeting shows a typed name or just "Welcome", which My Luma's greeting name already does.

## Not for us

- **novOS**: https://github.com/ConnysCode/novOS. A proof of concept that replaces LG's whole system UI on one webOS 25 model, including a quick settings panel that writes LG's settings. Those are system controls, which are ruled out.
- **LumaTV**: https://github.com/nphil/lumatv. A liquid-glass compositor mod for webOS 10.2. It's a separate project that overlaps with My Luma and has nothing new to borrow. Its "Instant on" option (staying in RAM while off) depends on LG power settings.
- **DV Freedom Launcher**: https://github.com/DVOpenLabs/dv-freedom-launcher. Its main build is Android alpha, derived from CleanYourTV, and the LG version is only a preview. Its "world packs" (offline artwork with credits) resemble the art-pack idea from 2026-11-18.
- **couch-tv 0.13.2 to 0.13.6 (to 65dbfc8)**: https://github.com/Seagey/couch-tv. Adds Ask AI with your own key, guest names from a bookings calendar, Philips Hue and Home Assistant through its bridge, and Bluetooth battery levels, which only work on Android. These are bridge-dependent or not for a single-user G5. Its fix for TVs that send a held OK as rapid up and down pairs doesn't seem needed on LG.
- **Aerial v3.1.0 and v3.1.1 panel protection and brightness (to fc229e5)**: https://github.com/aabytt/custom-screensaver-aerial. Adds custom brightness, gradual dimming and panel-off timers (system controls) and a fix for brightness being set too late. The font and solar ideas are listed above.
- **webosbrew catalogue**: no commits to webosbrew/apps-repo since 2026-11-17 (Home Chooser 0.2.0, which was already reported).
